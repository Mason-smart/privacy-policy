懒人修图 - 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 6 日

感谢您使用「懒人修图」（以下简称"本应用"）。我们非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明我们如何处理您的信息，特别是您在使用本应用过程中上传或处理的照片。

1. 我们收集的信息

1.1 您主动提供的照片

本应用的核心功能是图片编辑。您可以选择相册中的照片进行处理。这些照片是您主动选择并提供的。

1.2 我们不收集的信息

  • 我们不收集您的姓名、手机号、邮箱等个人身份信息。
  • 我们不收集您的位置信息、设备通讯录、短信等。
  • 我们不存储您编辑或导出的任何照片。

2. 照片的处理方式

2.1 本地处理

大部分编辑功能（裁剪、旋转、亮度、对比度、饱和度、滤镜、磨皮等）完全在您的设备本地完成，照片不会离开您的设备。

2.2 AI 抠图功能

当您使用"AI 抠图"功能时，为完成背景移除，您当前选择的照片会被临时上传至第三方服务 remove.bg（由 Kaleido AI GmbH 提供）进行处理。处理完成后：

  • 该照片不会被我们存储。
  • remove.bg 的隐私政策适用于其服务，详情请见：https://www.remove.bg/privacy
  • 如果您的照片包含敏感信息，请在使用 AI 抠图前谨慎考虑。

3. 数据存储

本应用只在您的设备本地保存以下内容（不会上传到我们的服务器）：

  • 您的个性化预设参数（滤镜、亮度等设置）
  • 您的 App 使用偏好

您可以通过卸载本应用来清除所有这些本地数据。

4. 权限说明

  • 相册/存储权限：用于让您选择照片进行编辑，以及保存编辑后的照片。
  • 网络权限：仅用于 AI 抠图功能与第三方服务通信。

5. 第三方服务

本应用使用以下第三方服务：

  • remove.bg（AI 抠图）
    网站：https://www.remove.bg
    隐私政策：https://www.remove.bg/privacy
  • Google AdMob（未来可能用于广告展示）
    隐私政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。我们不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。如果您是家长或监护人，发现您的孩子向我们提供了信息，请联系我们，我们会立即删除。

7. 政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的版本将在本页面公布，并更新"最后更新"日期。重大变更我们会在应用内通知。

8. 联系我们

Lazy Photo Editor - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Thank you for using "Lazy Photo Editor" (the "App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information, especially the photos you upload or process while using the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photos You Provide

The App's core function is photo editing. You may select photos from your gallery to process. These photos are voluntarily provided by you.

1.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, email, or other personal identity information.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS, or similar data.
  • We do not store any photos you edit or export.

2. How Photos Are Processed

2.1 Local Processing

Most editing features (crop, rotate, brightness, contrast, saturation, filters, smoothing, etc.) are performed entirely on your device. Photos never leave your device.

2.2 AI Background Removal

When you use the "AI Background Removal" feature, your currently selected photo is temporarily uploaded to a third-party service, remove.bg (provided by Kaleido AI GmbH), for background removal. After processing:

  • The photo is not stored by us.
  • remove.bg's privacy policy applies to their service: https://www.remove.bg/privacy
  • Please be cautious before using AI background removal if your photo contains sensitive information.

3. Data Storage

The App only stores the following locally on your device (not uploaded to our servers):

  • Your personalized preset parameters (filters, brightness, etc.)
  • Your app usage preferences

You can clear all this local data by uninstalling the App.

4. Permissions

  • Gallery/Storage: To let you pick photos for editing and save edited photos.
  • Network: Only used for the AI background removal feature to communicate with third-party services.

5. Third-Party Services

  • remove.bg (AI background removal)
    Website: https://www.remove.bg
    Privacy: https://www.remove.bg/privacy
  • Google AdMob (may be used for ads in the future)
    Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We will notify you in-app of any material changes.
